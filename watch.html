RIDEFRAME for Garmin

Run, ride, strength and more on the wrist: a preview of the watch app on a Forerunner 265 and a fenix 7X Pro, each at its real pixel size. The data pages and workouts come from the phone (Settings, Devices, Watch). Use the buttons around the watch, tap and swipe the screen, or the keyboard.

START · Enter
start, select, pause, set done, skip rest
UP / DOWN · ↑ ↓
scroll, next data page, change the value; hold for 10 kg; on the ready screen: the route
BACK · Esc
back; lap while recording; on a set: weight or reps; hold for the menu
Swipe left · ←
menu
Tap
a value to pick it, DONE to log the set
