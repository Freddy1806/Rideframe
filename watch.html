RIDEFRAME for Garmin

Strength on the wrist: a preview of the watch app on a Forerunner 265 and a fenix 7X Pro, each at its real pixel size. Use the buttons around the watch, tap and swipe the screen, or the keyboard.

START · Enter
start, select, set done, skip rest
UP / DOWN · ↑ ↓
scroll, change the value; hold for 10 kg
BACK · Esc
back; on a set: weight or reps; hold for the menu
Swipe left · ←
menu
Tap
a value to pick it, DONE to log the set
